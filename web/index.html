How to drive

  • W↑Accelerate
  • S↓Brake · hold when stopped to reverse
  • ADSteer
  • SpaceHandbrake
  • HHorn
  • CChase / driver's seat camera
  • DragLook around
  • RPut the bus back on the road
  • MSound on / off
  • EscPause
  • Keep left. Bangladesh drives on the left. Oncoming jeeps, CNGs and trucks use the right lane — hit one and the run is over.
  • Stop at every bus stop. Pull into the green zone on the left and come to a full stop. The doors open and passengers walk aboard.
  • Mind the edge. Striped bollards guard the drops. Scraping them costs points; leaving the road puts you back on it with a penalty.
  • Use the hills. The bus slows on climbs and runs away on descents — brake before the hairpins.
